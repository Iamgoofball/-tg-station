# Content Policy

## Intellectual Property Guidelines

This project does not accept contributions that include:

- Characters, names, or likenesses from copyrighted TV shows, films, or other media (including but not limited to Family Guy, The Simpsons, South Park, etc.)
- Direct references to trademarked intellectual property without transformative purpose
- Content that could expose the project to copyright or trademark infringement claims

## Why?

Adding copyrighted characters or IP-infringing content puts the project and its contributors at legal risk. Even fan projects can face takedown notices or legal action from IP holders.

## What We Accept

- Original characters and content created for this project
- Content inspired by themes (space, science fiction, etc.) without direct IP copying
- Parody content that is clearly transformative and does not copy protected expression

## Bounty Disclaimer

This project does not offer monetary bounties for contributions. Any issue claiming to offer payment for specific content additions should be treated with skepticism. Please verify with project maintainers before doing work based on bounty claims.

## Reporting IP Concerns

If you see existing content that may infringe on intellectual property rights, please open an issue with the `ip-concern` label.
