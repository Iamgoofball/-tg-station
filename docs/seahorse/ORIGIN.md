# Seahorse Origin

## Taxonomy

Seahorses belong to the genus *Hippocampus* — from Ancient Greek *hippos* (horse) and *kampos* (sea monster). They are fish, despite looking nothing like one.

## Evolutionary History

Seahorses evolved roughly 25 million years ago during the Miocene epoch. Their closest relatives are pipefish and seadragons (family Syngnathidae). The key evolutionary innovation? A curled prehensile tail and an upright posture — the only fish that swims vertically.

## Discovery

The ancient Greeks called them *hippokampos*, often depicted pulling Poseidon's chariot. In medieval bestiaries, seahorses were described as half-horse, half-fish creatures with magical properties.

## Modern Classification

There are 54 known species of seahorse, ranging from the tiny *Hippocampus satomiae* (less than 1 inch) to the massive *Hippocampus abdominalis* (over 12 inches). They are found in shallow tropical and temperate waters worldwide.
