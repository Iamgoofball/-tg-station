🎯 Objective
Implement a comprehensive anomalous entity containment and research framework within the SS13 Unreal Engine 5 codebase. This is not a simple "add more monsters" feature — it is a full-fidelity Anomalous Containment Wing (ACW) that functions as a self-contained department with its own staff, protocols, containment cells, research pipelines, and catastrophic breach scenarios.

The ACW must house a rotating catalog of anomalous entities — objects, creatures, and phenomena that violate the normal rules of SS13 physics. Each anomaly must have a unique containment protocol, a research tree that yields station-wide benefits, and a breach condition that threatens the entire round. The Wing must feel like a cross between an SCP Foundation site and a high-energy physics laboratory, staffed by Anomaly Researchers, Containment Engineers, and a new antag role: the Breach Cultist.

Containment is not optional. Breach is inevitable. The question is only when, and whether the station survives it.

🧠 Required Systems (Detailed Breakdown)
1. Anomaly Entity System
A modular entity framework supporting at least 12 initial anomaly types: Kinetic Entity (moves when unobserved), Void Leak (consumes matter in radius), Memetic Icon (alters perception of viewers), Temporal Loop Entity (repeats the last 30 seconds in its chamber), Gravity Well (localized gravity distortion), Phase Shifter (exists in two chambers simultaneously), Coral Growth (expands through walls if unchecked), Information Eater (deletes text from chat logs), Mirror Walker (emerges from reflective surfaces), Heat Sink (drains thermal energy), Sound Null (creates zone of silence), and Flesh Weaver (reconfigures organic matter).

Each anomaly has: containment requirements, breach triggers, research potential, and a unique visual/audio signature.

2. Containment Chamber Architecture
Modular containment chambers that must be constructed, maintained, and upgraded. Chambers require: reinforced walls (X tier), electromagnetic containment field generators, emergency purge systems (vent to space), observation windows with shutter controls, and automated turret systems for breach suppression. Chamber integrity degrades over time and requires Engineering maintenance.

3. Research Pipeline & Tech Tree
Anomaly Researchers (new job role) can study contained anomalies to unlock station-wide benefits: improved power efficiency (from studying the Heat Sink), localized gravity control (Gravity Well), matter transmutation (Void Leak byproducts), temporal acceleration for chemistry (Temporal Loop), and anti-memetic screening for command staff (Memetic Icon). Research requires specialized equipment: resonance scanners, containment field analyzers, and the dangerously unpredictable Anomaly Interaction Chamber.

4. Breach Protocol & Cascade System
When a containment chamber fails, the anomaly escapes into the station. Breach severity scales with chamber tier and anomaly type. A cascade system tracks multiple simultaneous breaches — two breaches create a "Containment Cascade" warning; three trigger "Wing Lockdown"; four or more initiate "Foundation Protocol" (full station evacuation order). Breach response teams (new ERT subtype) can be called.

5. Containment Engineer Role
A new engineering specialist responsible for chamber maintenance, field calibration, and emergency repair. Equipped with a containment hardsuit, portable field generator, and anomaly-resistant tools. Must balance routine maintenance with emergency response.

6. Breach Cultist Antagonist
A new antagonist whose objectives center on deliberately breaching containment: "Liberate the Void Leak," "Feed the Coral Growth until it reaches the Bridge," "Use the Mirror Walker to assassinate the Captain." Cultists gain anomaly-specific abilities after interacting with breached entities. They appear as normal crew until their first breach action.

7. Observation & Monitoring
A central ACW Monitoring Station with camera feeds for all chambers, integrity readouts, and automated alert system. The AI can observe and report but cannot directly control containment (enforced by ACW air-gap policy — a deliberate design constraint that creates interesting gameplay tension).

8. Narrative Integration
Anomalies have lore entries accessible via research consoles. Each has an origin story, containment history, and research notes. The ACW maintains a "Threat Level" display visible station-wide that rises with each breach, creating mounting narrative tension throughout the round.
