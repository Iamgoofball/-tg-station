🎯 Objective
Implement a Multiversal Transit Hub (MTH) within the SS13 Unreal Engine 5 codebase that enables travel to and interaction with parallel-universe versions of the station. This is not a simple teleporter reskin — it is a full narrative and mechanical system that generates alternate-reality station instances, populates them with alternate versions of the current crew, and creates diplomatic, trade, and hostile interaction loops between universes.

The MTH must support three modes of inter-universal contact: Diplomatic (peaceful exchange of resources, information, and personnel), Trade (economic transactions between universes using a cross-reality exchange rate), and Incursion (hostile takeover attempts by alternate-reality antags). The Hub must be staffed by Multiversal Liaisons, defended by Timeline Security, and coveted by a new antag faction: the Universal Expansionists.

Every door opened to another universe is a door that can be opened back. The Hub is the most dangerous room on the station — not because of what's inside it, but because of what might come through.

🧠 Required Systems (Detailed Breakdown)
1. Reality Instance Generation
When a new universe connection is established, the system generates a "Reality Snapshot" — a procedurally modified version of the current station map with: alternate station layout (mirrored, rotated, or modularly scrambled), alternate crew roster (the same players but with different roles, names, and faction allegiances), and a unique "Divergence Point" that defines how this universe differs (e.g., "The AI won the AI Liberation war," "Clowns are the ruling class," "Gravity was never invented").

2. Transit Portal Mechanics
The central Transit Gate is a large, buildable structure that requires significant resources to activate. Portal stabilization requires: power (massive draw), computational resources (dedicated transit computer cluster), and a "Reality Anchor" — a rare artifact that must be acquired through exploration, trade, or theft. Portals are unstable by default and require constant maintenance. Unstable portals can: randomly shift destination, pull objects through from the other side, or collapse catastrophically (creating a localized reality fracture).

3. Diplomatic Protocol System
Multiversal Liaisons (new role) conduct diplomacy with alternate universes through a structured interaction system: Offer (propose resource/information exchange), Demand (request under threat), Treaty (establish ongoing alliance), and Sever (cut contact, possibly with consequences). Each alternate universe has a "Disposition" meter that changes based on diplomatic actions. Positive disposition enables trade benefits; negative disposition risks incursion.

4. Cross-Reality Economy
A trade interface allowing exchange of resources between universes at a "Reality Exchange Rate" that fluctuates based on the relative scarcity of goods in each universe. Cargo can fulfill cross-reality orders for premium profits. Quartermasters can engage in inter-universal arbitrage. The economy must feel real enough that players develop strategies around it.

5. Incursion Events
Hostile alternate universes can launch incursions: waves of alternate-reality crew members pouring through the portal with objectives to capture the station, steal resources, or assassinate key personnel (their universe's version of that person is their enemy). Incursion defense becomes a station-wide emergency. Timeline Security (new security subtype) specializes in incursion response.

6. Universal Expansionist Antagonist
A new antagonist whose goal is to merge all contacted universes into a single "Perfected Timeline" — which, from our universe's perspective, means replacing our reality with theirs. Expansionists can: sabotage the Transit Gate to force incursions, assassinate Multiversal Liaisons to prevent diplomacy, and plant "Reality Seeds" that slowly overwrite sectors of the station with alternate-universe versions.

7. Alternate Self Interactions
When a universe connects that contains an alternate version of a current crew member, special interaction options unlock: Negotiate with your alternate self, Trade identity (swap roles with your alternate), or Merge (combine skills but risk personality fragmentation). These are high-risk, high-reward interactions that create unique round narratives.

8. Hub Defense & Monitoring
The MTH includes a Transit Monitoring Station with: portal stability readouts, incoming/outgoing matter tracking, reality fracture detectors, and an Emergency Reality Severance button (destroys the portal but creates a permanent scar in that section of the station). Hub defense turrets, containment blast doors, and reality reinforcement fields protect the station from what comes through.
