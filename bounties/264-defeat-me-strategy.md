### 2.2 The Rhythm of Destruction
Toolbox combat is not about spamming clicks. It is about rhythm:
- **Hit** (click) → **Step Left** → **Hit** → **Step Right** → **Hit**
- This serpentine movement pattern makes you harder to click while maintaining aggression
- NEVER backpedal in a straight line — you'll walk into their next swing

### 2.3 Target Priority
Aim for specific body zones by clicking the appropriate doll slot:
1. **Head** (first 3 hits): Causes brain damage, blurring their vision and slowing their reactions

3. **Left Leg** (if they try to run): Slow them down for the finishing sequence
4. **Chest** (finisher): Maximum surface area, reliable damage application

### 2.4 The Three-Swing Rule
Never stand still for more than three consecutive swings. After the third hit:
- Disengage by stepping diagonally (northeast, southeast, southwest, or northwest)
- This breaks their targeting rhythm
- Re-engage from a different angle
