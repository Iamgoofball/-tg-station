🎯 Objective
Implement a Chrono-Logical Operations Division (CLOD) within the SS13 Unreal Engine 5 codebase that introduces controlled time manipulation as a station resource — and uncontrolled temporal anomalies as a round-threatening hazard. This is not a simple "rewind" button; it is a full causality engine that allows the station to manipulate local time flow, but at the cost of accumulating "Temporal Paradox" that eventually manifests as reality-breaking timeline fractures.

The CLOD must operate a Temporal Manipulation Array (TMA) — a station-bound device capable of: local time dilation (speed up/slow down a room), temporal stasis (freeze an area in time), and limited chrono-reversion (rewind a small area by up to 60 seconds). Every use of the TMA generates Paradox Points. When Paradox exceeds threshold, Timeline Fractures spawn — hostile temporal entities that attack the station and attempt to "correct" the timeline by eliminating the crew responsible for the paradox.

Time is not a toy. The CLOD reminds everyone of this, usually posthumously.

🧠 Required Systems (Detailed Breakdown)
1. Temporal Manipulation Array (TMA)
A large, power-hungry station structure that must be assembled, calibrated, and operated by trained Chrono-Technicians (new role). The TMA has three operational modes:
- Local Time Dilation: Speed up or slow down time in a targeted room by a factor of 0.5x to 3x. Affects movement speed, chemical reactions, construction speed, and combat.
- Temporal Stasis Field: Freeze a designated area in time for 30-120 seconds. Entities inside are invulnerable but immobile. Requires continuous power draw.
- Chrono-Reversion: Rewind a small area (max 5x5 tiles) by 30-60 seconds. Restores objects, entities, and state. Cannot revive dead players (only "temporally displaced" ones). Highest Paradox cost.

2. Paradox Accumulation System
Each TMA operation generates Paradox Points (PP). PP generation scales with: area size affected, duration of effect, number of entities affected, and whether the operation "contradicts" a previously observed event. PP decays slowly over time (1 PP per 30 seconds of non-use). When PP exceeds the current Paradox Threshold (starts at 100, increases with TMA upgrades), a Timeline Fracture event triggers.

3. Timeline Fracture Entities
When Paradox overflows, the system spawns Timeline Fractures — hostile temporal entities that manifest in areas affected by recent TMA use. Fracture types include:
- Chrono-Wraith: A ghostly figure that moves in reverse-time and attacks crew by aging them (applies progressive debuffs).
- Paradox Duplicate: A hostile copy of a crew member from a "wrong" timeline that must be eliminated before it can merge with its original.
- Timeline Scar: A stationary zone of temporal instability that cycles rapidly through past/future states of that location, damaging anything inside.
- Causality Breaker: An entity that "un-happens" recent events in its vicinity — walls un-deconstruct, items un-pick-up, wounds un-heal. Extremely dangerous.

4. Chrono-Technician Role
New science department role responsible for: TMA operation, Paradox monitoring, fracture containment, and temporal calibration. Equipped with a Chrono-Suit (temporal hazard protection), Paradox Meter (handheld PP detector), and Temporal Stabilizer (tool for sealing timeline fractures). Must balance the station's desire for temporal assistance against the risk of fracture cascade.

5. Temporal Research Tree
Science can research TMA upgrades: reduced Paradox generation, increased effect radius, chrono-reversion for organic entities (high risk), and the dangerous "Temporal Loop Containment" that allows trapping a fracture entity for research. Each upgrade increases the Paradox Threshold, allowing more temporal manipulation before disaster.

6. Antagonist Temporal Exploitation
Traitors can purchase "Temporal Sabotage Kit" — devices that generate Paradox without using the TMA, forcing fractures without warning. Changeling "Temporal Echo" ability: after absorbing a victim, can briefly access their "timeline echo" to gain information. Cultists can attempt to summon "The End of Time" — a reality-ending entity that requires Paradox overflow to manifest.

7. Round-Narrative Integration
The TMA creates unique round narratives: "The Captain is dead — rewind the Bridge!", "Engineering needs 3x speed to repair the containment breach before it breaches!", "Someone keeps triggering fractures in Medbay and we don't know who." The system must generate memorable stories that players recount after the round.

8. Causality Log & Monitoring
A Chrono-Monitoring Console displays: current Paradox level, recent TMA operations and their PP cost, active timeline fractures with locations, and a "Causality Integrity" meter that represents the overall stability of the current timeline. When Causality Integrity reaches zero, the round enters "Temporal Collapse" — a final, unwinnable state where the timeline dissolves and the round ends.
