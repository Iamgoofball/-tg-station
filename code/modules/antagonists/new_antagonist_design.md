# Saboteur Antagonist Design Document

## Overview
The Saboteur is a new antagonist designed to disrupt station operations through sabotage and deception. This antagonist will challenge players to maintain station integrity while dealing with a cunning and unpredictable foe.

## Rationale
The Saboteur fits into the game's existing antagonist ecosystem by providing a new challenge for players. Their ability to sabotage systems and create confusion through deception adds a layer of complexity to the game's narrative and gameplay.

## Implementation Details

### Core Mechanics
1. **Sabotage**: The Saboteur can target and disable station systems, causing disruptions that players must address.
2. **Deception**: The Saboteur can create false alarms and misleading information to confuse players.
3. **Stealth**: The Saboteur must avoid detection while carrying out their objectives.

### Objectives
- Disrupt station operations through sabotage
- Create confusion through deception
- Avoid detection while achieving goals

### Spawn Conditions
- The Saboteur will spawn when there are more than 10 players on the station.
- There is a 20% chance of spawning when the conditions are met.

### Interactions
- The Saboteur will interact with players in subtle ways, making it difficult to identify them.
- Players may receive cryptic messages or experience strange occurrences when near the Saboteur.

## Balance Considerations
- The Saboteur's abilities should be balanced to ensure they pose a significant challenge without being overpowered.
- The frequency of sabotage and deception should be carefully tuned to maintain gameplay tension.

## Future Development
- Additional sabotage techniques could be added to increase the antagonist's complexity.
- More sophisticated deception tactics could be implemented to enhance player engagement.
