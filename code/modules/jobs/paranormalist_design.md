# Design Document: Paranormalist

## Rationale
In Space Station 13 rounds, when crew members die, they become ghosts (`/mob/dead/observer`). Aside from light poltergeist interactions (flicking lights, minor object spinning) or waiting to be revived/cloned/made into a cyborg, ghosts have very little direct, meaningful interaction with the living crew. 

The **Paranormalist** is designed to bridge this gap. As a Service department role, their primary duty is to detect, document, and interact with the spiritual energy on the station. By giving ghosts dedicated tools and items to communicate and interact with the Paranormalist, we enrich the gameplay of both dead players and the Paranormalist.

## Role & Fit
- **Department**: Service
- **Supervisor**: Head of Personnel (closely aligned with the Chaplain)
- **Playstyle**: Investigative, RP-heavy, and utility-oriented. While the Chaplain handles the religious/holy aspect of death, the Paranormalist uses pseudo-scientific tools to study death, ghosts, and ectoplasmic entities.

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Detection**: Using the **P.K.E. Meter**, the Paranormalist searches the station (especially maintenance and the morgue) for ghosts, dead bodies, and ectoplasmic residue left behind.
2. **Communication**: Using the **Ouija Board**, they can hold seances to ask the dead questions (e.g. "Who is the traitor?", "Where is the bomb?"). Ghosts can respond letter-by-letter.
3. **Companion/Containment**: With the **Spirit Jar**, the Paranormalist can allow friendly ghosts to occupy a glass jar. The ghost can float along with them and talk directly to the living crew, functioning as a ghostly assistant.
4. **Harvesting & Alchemy**: Using the **Ecto-Sucker**, they clean ectoplasm decals off the floor and bottle them. The ectoplasm reagent can be ingested by crew members to temporarily gain spectral sight, allowing them to see ghosts directly.

## Antagonist Fit
As an antagonist (e.g., Traitor), the Paranormalist has access to unique tools:
- They can gain intel directly from deceased crew members (who might or might not tell the truth!).
- Ectoplasm can be used to induce hallucinations or create glowing/spectral distractors.
- They can hide items inside the Spirit Jar or use it to smuggle items.
