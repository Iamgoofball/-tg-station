**Space Station 13 Potato Wedges**

Ingredients:
- 4 large russet potatoes (about 2 pounds)
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 425°F.
2. Scrub the potatoes clean, then cut each into 8 wedges (like a pizza, but potato).
3. In a big bowl, toss the wedges with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, pepper, and salt until well coated.
4. Spread the wedges in a single layer on a baking sheet. Don't crowd 'em—give 'em room to crisp up.
5. Bake for 25 minutes, then flip each wedge with a spatula. Sprinkle Parmesan on top if you're feeling fancy.
6. Bake another 15–20 minutes until golden brown and crispy on the outside, fluffy inside.
7. Serve hot alongside your favorite space burger or synth-meat steak.

These wedges are a hit in the mess hall—no metric conversion required! Enjoy, and remember: a full crew is a happy crew.
